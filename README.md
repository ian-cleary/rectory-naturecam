# Rectory Naturecam

Wildlife caught by the NatureSpy trail camera ("NatureBinky") in the garden at
The Old Rectory, Manton — 16 May to 2 July 2026.

Live site: https://ian-cleary.github.io/rectory-naturecam/

## What is here

- `index.html` — the whole site: searchable species catalogue, the "big night"
  of 17–18 June, about the camera, and tips for helping the visitors.
- `images/` — 22 trail-camera frames, cropped to the animal and resized.
- `build_site.py` — the generator. The site renders from the classification
  database at `~/workspace/goals/garden-nature-camera-photo-analysis-100heltc/hidden_files/classifications.csv`
  (2,775 files). Re-run it to rebuild `index.html` after any re-classification.

## The numbers

2,775 files recorded; 250 wildlife files; 12 species; 27 video clips.
Regular visitors: woodpigeon, blackbird, pheasant, magpie, carrion crow,
song thrush, small bird (unidentified). Unusual visitors: owl, hedgehog,
muntjac deer, roe deer, red fox.

Household captures (people, the dog, the cats) are not shown. Two
identifications from the first pass did not survive a full viewing and are
not listed: a "badger" video that is the dog by the willow, and a "grey heron"
video that is the dog in the vegetation. Both are noted in the site's
identification notes.

## Videos

Video clips are hosted on Google Drive and linked from the site
(`https://drive.google.com/file/d/<id>/view`). They are not re-hosted here.
Public visitors need link sharing enabled on the Drive folder
`100HELTC` for the clips to play.
